# Market hours displayed by QuantFarm AI

The interface displays each market's current local time, regular session and the corresponding Alberta time using IANA time zones and automatic daylight-saving conversion.

- NYSE/Nasdaq: 09:30–16:00 America/New_York
- TSX: 09:30–16:00 America/Toronto
- London Stock Exchange: 08:00–16:30 Europe/London
- Euronext: 09:00–17:30 Europe/Paris
- Tokyo Stock Exchange: 09:00–11:30 and 12:30–15:30 Asia/Tokyo
- ASX: 10:00–16:00 Australia/Sydney
- Forex: Sunday 17:00 to Friday 17:00 America/New_York
- Crypto: 24/7

Regular schedules do not replace official holiday and early-close calendars. Live-data entries are blocked outside regular sessions when the safety setting is enabled.
